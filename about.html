Our Mission

"A space where patients find trusted doctors, and doctors share genuine knowledge — without fighting for visibility through paid advertising."

KCS - Knowledge Contribution Score

Doctors earn KCS through: articles (10pts), answering questions (2pts), conferences (50pts), citations (5pts), endorsements (20pts).

RCS - Resource Contribution Score

Clinics earn RCS through: patient ratings, doctor count, community programs, facility quality.
